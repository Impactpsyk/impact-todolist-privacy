Privacy Policy for Impact To Do List

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Impact To Do List is developed by Impact Group. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled within the app. Impact To Do List is designed to store data locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export, back up, sync, or share it through app features.

Local-First Design

Impact To Do List is a local-first application. App data — including tasks, notes, checklists, priorities, categories, lists, locations, focus sessions, To Pay records, people, attachments, settings, and history — is stored locally on the user's device using a local SQLite database and secure storage.

No cloud database, remote backend, or server-side storage is used for app data.

Data leaves the device in the following circumstances:

  • When the user explicitly initiates an export, backup, sync, or share action
  • When the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) communicates with Google's servers
  • When map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap servers
  • When reverse geocoding is performed through the device's platform geocoder
  • When Wi-Fi sync exchanges data with a paired device on the local network

Data Types

Impact To Do List processes user-entered data including:

  • Tasks: titles, notes, priorities, statuses, due dates, reminders, categories, checklists, favorites, GPS locations
  • Focus sessions: timer data, session history, productivity scores
  • To Pay records: payment amounts, categories, due dates
  • People: names used for task assignment
  • Lists and groups: organizational containers for tasks
  • Locations: named places with optional GPS coordinates
  • Attachments: photos captured or selected by the user
  • Settings: app preferences, language, theme, notification configuration
  • History: completed task records, activity log

All data is entered and controlled by the user.

Data Usage

Impact Group does not sell personal information. Impact To Do List does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

The app includes advertising services as described in the Advertising section below.

Advertising

Impact To Do List displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App information (app version, bundle ID)
  • Advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID where available)
  • IP address (used by Google for ad serving and geographic targeting)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent

Where required by applicable law (including GDPR in the EEA), the app presents the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before requesting ads. Users may:

  • Accept personalized advertising
  • Accept non-personalized advertising only
  • Decline advertising where legally permitted

Users can change their advertising preferences at any time through Settings > Privacy & Ads > Privacy Options.

Ad Placement

Banner ads appear on passive browsing screens including the Dashboard, Tasks, Calendar, To Pay, Statistics, Completed History, Kanban, Search, Status, Activity, People, Groups, and Map screens. Banner ads do not appear during task creation, editing, Focus sessions, GPS selection, backup/restore, export, sync, donation, settings, or privacy consent flows.

Additionally, the app may display up to one full-screen interstitial advertisement per calendar day. This interstitial appears only after a meaningful user action has successfully completed (such as completing a task, marking a payment complete, or stopping a focus session). The interstitial never appears at app startup, on navigation between screens, on app resume, on automatic timer expiry, or after file export. If no suitable opportunity arises, or if the ad is unavailable, no interstitial is shown that day.

Location Data

The app requests location permission when the user chooses to:

  • Attach GPS coordinates to a task
  • Use the map picker to select a location
  • Track current location for location-based task reminders

Location data is stored locally on the device. The app uses ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION permissions. Location is requested only when the user explicitly initiates a location feature.

When GPS coordinates are captured, the app uses the device's built-in reverse geocoding service to convert coordinates to a human-readable address. On Android, this uses Google's geocoding service, which receives the latitude and longitude to determine the address. The resulting address text is stored locally.

Map Services

When the user opens the mini-map feature, the app loads map tiles from external services:

  • unpkg.com — for the Leaflet map library
  • tile.openstreetmap.org — for OpenStreetMap tiles

These requests expose the device's IP address and requested map area to those external servers. No personal data or task content is transmitted to these services.

Camera and Photos

The app requests camera permission for:

  • QR code scanning (Wi-Fi sync pairing)
  • Task photo attachments

Photos are stored locally on the device. No images are uploaded to external servers.

Microphone and Voice Notes

The app requests microphone permission when the user records a voice note for a task attachment. Voice recordings are stored locally on the device. No audio data is uploaded to external servers.

Notifications

The app uses local scheduled notifications for:

  • Task due date reminders
  • Overdue alerts
  • Daily summary
  • Sync notifications
  • Focus session alerts
  • To Pay reminders

Notifications are scheduled and delivered locally using the device's notification system. No push notification server is used.

Wi-Fi Sync

The app supports optional device-to-device synchronization over a local Wi-Fi network:

  • Sync requires explicit QR code pairing between two devices
  • All sync traffic is encrypted with AES-256-GCM
  • Authentication is required for every sync exchange
  • Unauthenticated requests are rejected
  • The pairing secret never travels over the network — it is exchanged only through the QR code (out-of-band)
  • Sync data does not leave the local network
  • No cloud relay or external server is involved

Export and Backup

The user can export task data in the following formats:

  • PDF
  • XLSX (Excel)
  • DOCX (Word)
  • Share (Android share sheet)
  • Save (file system)
  • Print

Exported files are generated locally and shared only when the user explicitly initiates the action.

Data Retention

All app data is retained locally on the device until the user deletes it. The app provides:

  • Individual task deletion
  • Completed history viewing and restoration
  • Clear Cache (removes cached data; preserves tasks, settings, and history)
  • Clear History (removes completed task history)
  • Factory Reset (removes all app data)

Impact Group does not retain any user data because no server-side storage exists.

Data Security

  • Task and settings data is stored in a local SQLite database
  • Sensitive values (device identity, sync pairing keys) are stored using a combination of secure device storage and the local database
  • Wi-Fi sync uses AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption
  • Release builds are signed with the official Impact Group certificate

Users are responsible for maintaining device security (screen lock, software updates).

Children

Impact To Do List is not directed at children under 13 years of age. Impact Group does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Donation and Support

Impact To Do List includes an optional donation feature that allows users to support the project through PayPal or PromptPay. Donations are entirely voluntary and do not unlock any app features.

Payment processing is handled by the respective external payment service (PayPal or the user's banking app for PromptPay). Impact Group does not receive or store credit card numbers, bank account details, or payment credentials through the app. The app opens the external payment service when the user initiates a donation.

User Rights

Users have full control over their data within the app. Under applicable privacy laws, users may have the right to:

  • Access their data (visible directly in the app)
  • Delete their data (available through app controls)
  • Export their data (available through export features)
  • Opt out of personalized advertising (available through Settings > Privacy & Ads)

No Sale of Personal Information

Impact Group does not sell, rent, lease, or trade personal information to third parties.
